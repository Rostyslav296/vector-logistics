Meet Rusty

I've been where you are

I'm Rusty, the owner of Vector Logistics. Before I ever dispatched anyone else's truck, I was an owner-operator with my own MC and DOT numbers.

I found my own loads on all the top load boards and from direct shippers. I negotiated with the brokers, handled my own bills of lading and worked with factoring companies to get paid. I know what it's like to sit at a truck stop refreshing a load board when you should be sleeping.

That's why I started Vector Logistics: to give owner-operators a dispatcher who understands the business because he has run one, and who works to get you the best dollar per mile on every load.

How we work

Plain dealing, the way I'd want to be treated as a carrier

Everything is agreed in writing before your first load.

You keep the final say

There is no forced dispatch. We bring you the load and the rate, and you decide whether to take it.

We represent you

Our job is getting you the best rate, not keeping a broker happy. We're your dispatcher, not a freight broker.

A fee you can see

You pay 6% of each load's gross, agreed in writing before we start. No contract and no setup fee.

Local and accountable

A Tennessee business you can call, with a real person who answers and knows your truck.
