Questions

What carriers ask us

Something else on your mind? Ask us.

Are you a freight broker?

No. A broker works for the shipper; a dispatcher works for you, the carrier. The rate confirmation is always between you and the broker, and we never take money from a broker or a shipper. Our only pay is our fee from you.

What does it cost?

6% of each load's gross: the rate confirmation amount, fuel surcharge included. Detention, layover, TONU and lumper reimbursements are yours. There is no setup fee and no contract, and if we don't book you a load, you pay nothing.

Do I have to take every load you find?

No. There is no forced dispatch. We bring you the load, the rate and the route, and you say yes or no.

Do you work with new authorities?

Yes. The first months on a new MC are when good freight is hardest to find, because many brokers want history you don't have yet. We know which brokers work with new authorities.

What do I need to get started?

Your MC authority letter, certificate of insurance and a W-9, plus your equipment, home base, lanes and the rate per mile you need. We send the broker setup packets for you.

How do I pay the fee?

We invoice every Friday for the loads delivered that week, due in 7 days, by ACH, Zelle or check. If you use a factoring company, it can pay us for you when you set that up.

Where are you located?

Vector Logistics is based in Tennessee and American-owned. We dispatch trucks across the lower 48 states, from your home base and on your lanes.
