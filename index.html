Everything off your plate but the wheel

Searching boards and calling brokers from a truck stop costs you miles and money. Hand us the office work.

Load sourcing

We work the leading load boards, including DAT, Truckstop and 123Loadboard, along with direct shippers, to find freight for your truck and your lanes.

Rate negotiation

We call the brokers and push for the best rate per mile on every load, because a good first offer is rarely the best one.

Route and backhaul planning

We plan each load around the next one, cutting deadhead miles and keeping your truck headed where the freight pays.

Paperwork and setup

Broker setup packets, W-9s, certificates of insurance, MC letters and rate confirmations, filled out and sent for you.

Factoring and billing help

We send bills of lading and paperwork to your factoring company, so the money from each load reaches you without delay.

A real person on the phone

When a load changes or a broker goes quiet, you call your dispatcher, someone who knows your truck, and they handle it.

How it works

Getting started takes one phone call.

Tell us about your truck

Your equipment, your lanes, your home time and what you need to earn per mile.

Send your documents

Your MC authority letter, certificate of insurance and W-9. We set you up with brokers.

We find and negotiate

We bring you the best loads we can find, at the best rate we can get. You say yes or no.

You drive and get paid

We send the rate confirmation and route, then handle the paperwork to your factoring company.
